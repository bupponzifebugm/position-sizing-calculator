Algo Trade bot picks are the FIRST filter. Read in this exact order. Any INSTANT FAIL = Skip immediately.
  • Bot Score: Must be ≥ 80. Below 75 is an instant fail. Do not rationalize a 74.
  • Stage: Must be EARLY_SETUP or EARLY_CONFIRM. Extended = skip.

  • Bandar Net: Must be > +0.1% of market cap. Negative = no conviction.
  • Top 3 Buyers: Look for AK, CC, XL, OD, MU. (MG dominant alone = goreng warning).
  • YP Position: YP must NOT be in the top 3 buyers (retail driven = late signal).
  • Volume: Must be ≥ 1.5x above 5-day average.

2. Execution & TP Protocol
  • Morning Routine: Wake 08:00. Check USD/IDR at 08:30. Determine Risk %. Assess Wall Street/News. Focus < 7 = No Trade.
  • Entry: Enter ONLY if price enters pre-marked zone. No chasing ever.
  • Stop Loss: Placed IMMEDIATELY after order fill. Never move SL further from entry. Never average down.
  • Take Profit 1: At +7.5% hit, sell exactly 40%. IMMEDIATELY move SL to breakeven. (Trade is now free).
  • Take Profit 2: At +18% hit, sell 40%. (Locks in 2:1 R:R target).
  • Runner: Trail the remaining 20% below each higher swing low. Exit on breakdown.
  • Hard Close: ALL positions flat at 09:30 on class days, 11:00 on free days.
